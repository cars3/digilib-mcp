How it works

Four steps, one continuous flow inside your conversation.

No new tabs, no copy-pasting DOIs. Just tell Digilib what you're researching.

Extract

Search papers and books across OpenAlex, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, Open Library and Google Books — all at once.

Use

Keywords get sharpened by an LLM pass; results are deduplicated and normalized before they reach your chat.

Cite

Name a format — APA, MLA, Chicago, whatever your paper needs — and get a real, properly built citation.

Export

Get finished LaTeX for the whole bibliography — ready to render or drop straight into your paper.

The three tools

Everything Digilib exposes to your model.

search_papers

Search papers

Fans out across OpenAlex, arXiv, Semantic Scholar and Crossref for keywords, context and a year range.

search_books

Search books

Queries Open Library and Google Books, then normalizes and deduplicates results by title.

export_research_tool

Export research

Builds real LaTeX for your summary, recommended literature and liked articles, in any citation format.
